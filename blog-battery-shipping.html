UN38.3 and shipping lithium: what importers should plan early

Lithium-ion and LiFePO₄ packs are classified as dangerous goods for transport. Rules evolve; always verify with your freight forwarder before booking.

Documentation pack

Typically you will need test summaries (for example UN38.3), MSDS, and sometimes country-specific import declarations. Ask your supplier for a document list tied to the exact SKU and batch.

Sea versus air

Sea freight is common for integrated packs in steel cases. Air freight may face stricter state-of-charge limits and packaging tests — plan extra lead time.

Labeling on arrival

Ensure outer carton labels match the shipper’s dangerous goods declaration so customs and warehouse teams can receive without delay.
