Building a bill of materials for containerized solar imports

When you plan a full container of PV modules, landed cost is driven by more than price per watt. Use this outline when comparing quotations from suppliers such as Qingdao Vethy Industrial Co., Ltd.

1. Mechanical and electrical baseline

Confirm cell type, module dimensions, frame height, and connector brand. Mismatched frame heights can break your racking compatibility across batches.

2. Palletization and 40HQ math

Ask for pieces per pallet and pallets per high-cube container. Small differences in stacking rules change how many megawatts you actually land per shipment.

3. Warranty and flash test policy

Linear degradation tables and eligibility for replacement versus repair should be summarized in your internal SKU file so your sales team quotes consistently.
