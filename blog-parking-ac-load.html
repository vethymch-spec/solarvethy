Sizing house batteries for overnight parking AC runtime

Parking air conditioners for trucks and RVs draw significant DC current through an inverter stage or native DC compressor, depending on model. Integrators should combine catalog cooling power with realistic duty cycle.

Start from electrical input

Request the manufacturer’s input power curve at your expected ambient temperature. Peak current matters for BMS and fuse sizing, not only average overnight consumption.

Depth of discharge

LiFePO₄ packs are often specified for 80–90% usable window. Leave margin for morning inverter losses and auxiliary loads such as lighting and pumps.

Solar recharge

If the vehicle carries a PV array, match MPPT voltage windows with the pack nominal voltage. Solar panels from the same supplier ecosystem can simplify documentation for your customer.
