Parking air conditioner

Also known as truck sleeper AC or RV parking AC, these roof-mounted units cool the cab or living space while the engine is off — essential for driver rest, fuel saving, and compliance with idle restrictions.

Why fleets and RV owners choose parking AC

Traditional idling wastes fuel and increases maintenance. A dedicated parking AC powered from the vehicle battery bank (often lithium) or a small generator interface delivers quieter sleep with predictable energy use.

  • Low-noise compressors suitable for overnight parking
  • Roof profiles designed for common truck and RV cut-out patterns
  • Coordination with our battery line for Ah/hour load planning
  • Spare parts philosophy discussed per model for your market
